## How to play

- **WASD / Arrow keys:** move
- **Stand still:** auto-aim and shoot the nearest enemy in range

Enemies swarm in from every side. Run to make space, plant your feet, and fire. Kill enemies to collect coins, then spend them in the shop on damage, health, movement speed, and reload speed after you die.
